01_PROJECT_CONTEXT
===================

PRODUCT
-------
JalLekha ("jal" = water, "lekha" = ledger) is a web application that
reconciles water delivery and consumption records for campus facilities
teams and housing-society maintenance teams.

Product promise: "Know what came in, what was measured, and what needs
checking."

THE PROBLEM
-----------
Campus and society maintenance staff keep tanker delivery receipts, tank
dipstick readings and cumulative meter logs in separate registers. When the
numbers disagree, someone traces entries by hand and cannot easily tell:

  - a duplicate receipt   -> needs a record correction
  - a missing reading     -> needs a measurement
  - an entry/unit error   -> needs a correction
  - a real imbalance      -> needs physical inspection

Spreadsheets total numbers but do not flag duplicates, do not treat missing
readings as missing, carry no measurement uncertainty, and keep no review
history. The local frequency and scale of the problem remain hypotheses
until the team runs a permissioned pilot; the application is honest about
this everywhere.

WHAT JALLEKHA DOES
------------------
Staff record deliveries, tank stock readings and cumulative meter values
(manually or via validated CSV import). A deterministic engine computes the
water balance for one tank over a chosen period:

    R = O + D - U - C

  O = opening stock at the period start instant
  D = recorded inflows inside [start, end)
  U = measured outflow (cumulative meter delta across the same instants)
  C = closing stock at the period end instant

and reports a conservative uncertainty interval built from the DECLARED
measurement uncertainty of every input. Findings (duplicate candidates,
missing readings, invalid values, residuals outside uncertainty) become
owned verification cases with evidence, reviewable resolutions, and a full
audit trail. Corrections preserve history; recalculations supersede rather
than overwrite previous runs.

USERS
-----
  Primary operator : facilities manager / society maintenance supervisor
  Data entry       : enters receipts and readings, works assigned cases
  Reviewer         : checks proposed corrections, records outcomes
  Visitor/judge    : uses the clearly-labeled synthetic demo, no login

NON-NEGOTIABLES (do not weaken these in any change)
---------------------------------------------------
N1. A missing required reading means NO residual is asserted. Never
    substitute zero, never borrow a nearby reading (tolerance is 0 in the
    shipped rule version), never treat a gap as a discrepancy by itself.
N2. A discrepancy is never worded as proof of theft, leakage, or any cause.
    The engine and UI state this explicitly.
N3. Uncertainty is a conservative sum of DECLARED input bounds, never an
    invented confidence score, never root-sum-square, never hidden.
N4. Corrections never destroy history: voids keep the record with a reason,
    new runs supersede old ones, and every mutation writes an audit event.
N5. The public demo uses synthetic data and says so on every screen; user
    ("real") data lives only in the operator's own browser unless a shared
    backend is deliberately added (see 05_BACKLOG).
N6. The engine works with AI completely off. AI features are opt-in,
    use the operator's own key, and must fail safely to rule-based output.
N7. Numbers shown in the UI always come from the domain result or persisted
    records. No simulated, animated-to-different, or decorative values.
